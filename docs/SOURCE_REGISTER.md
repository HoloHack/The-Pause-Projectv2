## Explicitly rejected claim

The older "57% stopped within ten seconds" observation pertains to in-person school bullying episodes and must **not** be represented as the effectiveness of online bystander choices. Our chapter instead provides qualitative guidance without success estimates.

## Interpretation rules

- Distinguish estimates from universal facts.
- Display the denominator / age range and timing beside percentages.
- Never add overlapping period figures together.
- Never present this symbolic network as a scientific prediction.
- Update the study register if newer eSafety evidence is used.
